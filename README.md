## 🇨🇳 中文

根据剧情与参考素材，制作 30–90 秒、16:9 横屏的日本 90 年代二维赛璐璐 City Pop 都市动画短片，完成连续性资产、分段分镜、视频、声音与剪辑。先建立 Style Master（手绘轮廓线、分层赛璐璐阴影、图形化手绘都市背景、克制的模拟介质质感）并锁定时间轴，再按剧情段落逐段生成，保持已确认的人物身份、城市空间、视觉语言与时间轴一致。不用于静图、简单图生视频、纯字幕、完整长 MV、二次元游戏 PV、无叙事普通动效或超过 90 秒的项目。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 剧情、人物/场景参考或可选音频 → 一条 30–90 秒、16:9 横屏完整短片
- 时间轴锁定：30–45 秒约 3–4 段，46–60 秒 4–5 段，61–90 秒 5–7 段，每段通常 10–15 秒
- Style Master 锁死赛璐璐动画语言、手绘轮廓、分层阴影、色彩方向、光线逻辑与时代质感，不叠加冲突风格
- 把参考视频当可复用画风包：多层视差、水平运动模糊、卡点硬切、沉稳跟拍与大透视建立镜头可转译成规则
- 连续性资产覆盖人物身份、轮廓、脸部、发型、服装、年龄特征与比例，场景保持建筑、地理与空间结构
- 逐段分析事件、冲突、情绪、结尾与节拍；失败按已诊断原因只重试一次，仍失败再换兼容模型

## 🇬🇧 English

Produces a 30–90 second, 16:9 Japanese 1990s cel-shaded City Pop city animation short from a story plus reference assets, covering continuity assets, segmented storyboards, video, sound and editing. It first builds a Style Master — hand-drawn contour lines, layered cel shading, graphic hand-painted urban backdrops, restrained analogue texture — and locks the timeline before any asset is made. Each story segment maps to one production shot and one video segment, so character identity, city space, visual language and timing stay consistent. It is not for still images, simple image-to-video, subtitles only, full-length MVs, anime game PVs, non-narrative motion or projects over 90 seconds.

### ✨ What it does

- Story, character/scene references or optional audio → a complete 30–90s 16:9 short
- Timeline lock: 3–4 segments at 30–45s, 4–5 at 46–60s, 5–7 at 61–90s, usually 10–15s each
- Style Master freezes cel-animation language, hand-drawn contours, layered shading, colour direction, lighting logic and period texture — no conflicting styles stacked on top
- Reference videos become a reusable style pack: multi-layer parallax, horizontal motion blur, hard beat cuts, steady tracking and big-perspective establishing shots
- Continuity assets cover identity, silhouette, face, hair, costume, age traits and proportions; scenes keep architecture, geography and spatial structure
- Per-segment analysis of events, conflict, emotion, ending and beats; one retry on a diagnosed failure, then a compatible model
